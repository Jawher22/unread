Packing for a week of rain
Most of us pack for the weather we hope for, not the weather we get.
This guide covers what actually kept us dry on a wet week in the hills.
The short version is that layers beat one heavy coat, and dry socks matter more than anything else you carry.
Key takeaways
• Pack two thin layers
• Bring three pairs of socks
• Leave the umbrella at home
