The lamp room is the glazed room at the top of a lighthouse that holds the light and its lens.[1] Keepers spent most of each night in it, trimming wicks and winding the clockwork.

History

[edit]

Early lamp rooms burned whale oil and filled with soot within hours.[2] According to some keepers,[who?] the smoke was worse than the cold.

Equipment

[edit]

A typical lamp room held a handful of standard instruments:

  • Fresnel lens
  • Fog bell
  • Paraffin vapour burner

Electric lamps replaced most of them during the twentieth century, and few keepers remained after that.
