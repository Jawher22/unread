# start: Most text on a screen
Most text on a screen was never meant to be heard.
This post explains how we turned a page into something a voice can read without stumbling.
Why it matters
A good reader skips what a listener does not need.
Menus, buttons and timestamps are noise, and earlier attempts read all of it aloud.
Here is the smallest version of the idea.
The rest of this post is about everything that one line hides.
Listening should take less effort than reading.
• Find where the article starts.
• Skip everything that is not the article.
That is the whole trick, and it took us a year to get right.
