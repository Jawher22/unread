Lamp room
The lamp room is the glazed room at the top of a lighthouse that holds the light and its lens.
Keepers spent most of each night in it, trimming wicks and winding the clockwork.
History
Early lamp rooms burned whale oil and filled with soot within hours.
According to some keepers, the smoke was worse than the cold.
Equipment
A typical lamp room held a handful of standard instruments:
• Fresnel lens
• Fog bell
• Paraffin vapour burner
Electric lamps replaced most of them during the twentieth century, and few keepers remained after that.
