The night train from the coast used to arrive forty minutes late on most mornings. Our first report(opens in a new window) blamed the signals, but the real cause was the timetable itself.

Copy linkWhy it slipped

The old timetable gave the train two minutes at each of twelve small stations, and nobody could board that fast.

Copy linkWhat we changed

We now let the train wait longer at the four busiest stations and skip two that almost nobody uses at night.

Since the change, it has arrived on time on all but three mornings. Interested in timetables? Our planning team is hiring.
