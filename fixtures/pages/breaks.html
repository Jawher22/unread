Notes from the lighthouse

Editor's note: this piece first ran in the harbour newsletter. Read the original issue.

The keeper climbs the stairs every evening at dusk.

She counts one hundred and twelve steps on the way up.
Nobody has ever counted them on the way down.

The lamp turns twice a minute.
Ships see it from twenty miles out.
On foggy nights the horn takes over.

Winter

In winter the supply boat comes once a fortnight.
