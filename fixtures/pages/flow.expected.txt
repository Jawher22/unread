# start: The island ferry
The island ferry
The ferry leaves at half past seven every weekday morning.
Bicycles ride free with a day pass bought before boarding.
Tickets are sold by Harbour Lines at the pier office.
We stopped at Ardmore, Port Ellen, Bowmore, Port Askaig, Craighouse, Tayvallich and Crinan before turning home.
A tide table lists the HW times for each day.
Spring tides come twice a month.
