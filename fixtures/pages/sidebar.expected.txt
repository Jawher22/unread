# start: Lanterns on the canal
Lanterns on the canal
Every autumn the canal society hangs paper lanterns along the towpath for one long weekend.
The lanterns need three things to survive the night:
• Dry paper that will not sag in the evening mist.
• Candles short enough to stay below the paper.
• A hook that holds when the wind comes off the water.
Why the candles kept going out
The first year, half of the candles went out before nine, because the lanterns hung too low over the water.
