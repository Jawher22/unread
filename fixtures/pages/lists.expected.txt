Packing for the hut
We weighed three options before the trip.
Carry everything up in one long day.
Split the load over two trips.
Send the heavy bags with the mule.
The mule won, as it usually does.
Seventh rule: never feed the mule apples.
Eighth rule: always thank the mule.
• Bring a spare torch.
