"You're late," Mara said, without looking up from the map.
"The bridge was out," Tom answered. "I had to go around by the mill."
She folded the map in half and finally met his eyes.
"Then we've lost the morning. Did anyone see you?"
He hesitated, just long enough for her to notice.
"Only the miller's boy," he said. "And he won't talk. I'm almost sure of it."
